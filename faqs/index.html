The Experience

Hotshots brings a real airsoft target range to your private venue. Your group shoots replicas at electronic targets over four timed rounds, ending in a head-to-head final. You only ever shoot at targets, never at people. We bring and set up everything; you provide the space.

Two sessions, both £305 for the whole group. Static Range — up to 12 people, electric rifle replicas, two shooters at a time from a 5m firing line, on AttackSense electronic targets. Dynamic Range — up to 6 people, electric pistol replicas, one shooter at a time on six electronic targets and a Texas Star, walking forward from 5m to 3m (stop, then shoot). Both have four rounds that get harder each time, ending in a head-to-head final, with a live leaderboard.

Every session is 2 hours of range time, and you can add an extra hour. We arrive before the start to set up.

Every session is fully briefed and supervised by a qualified instructor. You can read about our founder on the About page.

Equipment

No. We bring everything — replicas, BBs, targets and safety glasses. Just bring photo ID: everyone shooting must be 18 or over.

AttackSense is an electronic target system. It registers each hit, keeps score in real time and shows a live leaderboard, so everyone can see how they're doing throughout the session.

You only ever shoot at targets — never at people. Every session starts with a safety brief and is supervised throughout by a qualified instructor. Every replica is power-tested with a chronograph before use and kept at or below 1.14 joules, under the UK legal limits. Safety glasses are compulsory inside the range, and all shooting is contained by the gazebo walls and a backstop.

After you book, we'll talk through your venue with you so your session runs without a hitch. Not sure about your venue? Talk to us first — send a photo of the space and we'll tell you if it works.
