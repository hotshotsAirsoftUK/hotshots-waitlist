Two Sessions. Pick Your Style.

Every session is 2 hours, with an optional extra hour. Live scoring on AttackSense electronic targets. A head-to-head final. £305 for the whole group.

Static Range
Up to 12 people.
Electric rifle replicas from a 5m firing line, two shooters at a time, on AttackSense electronic targets. Precision-focused. Real-time scoring. Competitive without the chaos.
Dynamic Range
Up to 6 people.
Electric pistol replicas, one shooter at a time, on six electronic targets and a Texas Star. Walk forward from 5m to 3m — stop, then shoot. Speed and accuracy under pressure.
